→THE LOOP

A four-stage circuit, not a character flaw.

Every relapse follows the same neural sequence. The window to intervene is the gap between craving and ritual — measured in seconds, not minutes.
STAGE 01

Cue

Notification. Boredom. A specific room. Wi-Fi reconnecting at 11 PM.

EXTERNAL · <1s
→
STAGE 02

Craving

Dopamine anticipation spike. Subjective urge climbs toward peak.

PEAKS · ~90s
→
STAGE 03 — 04

Ritual & reward

Phone unlock, scroll, consume, numb. Loop closes. The pattern strengthens.

AUTOMATIC · UNCONSCIOUS
The intervention window is between Stage 02 and Stage 03. Most therapy operates after Stage 04 — interpreting damage, not interrupting it.

→THE STATE MACHINE

A locked 10-minute wave. Three phases. No skip.

Designed around the 90-second neurological half-life of an acute craving. The wave outlasts the spike.
PHASE 01

Somatic interruption

Body / cold / stillness. A physical task pulls the patient out of the trance loop.

0:00 — 3:00  ·  180 sec
→
PHASE 02

Cognitive hijack

AI-validated task or self-confrontation. The pre-frontal cortex is forced back online.

3:00 — 8:00  ·  300 sec
→
PHASE 03

Cooldown

Box breathing, four cycles. The autonomic system settles before the patient is released.

8:00 — 10:00  ·  ≥64 sec
No abandon button. Closing the app mid-wave logs a failure on resume.
No fast-forward. Phases gate on real-time elapsed, not taps.
No partial credit. Either the system clears the wave, or it doesn't.

→PHASE 01 / 0:00 — 3:00

Somatic interruption.

The reflex lives in the body. We pull it out of the screen and back into physical space — the patient chooses the modality, not the avoidance.
01 / BODY

Push-up set አካል

Resilience-scaled, validated by tap-count. Re-routes blood, breath, and attention away from the dopamine pathway.

02 / COLD

Cold water ቀዝቃዛ

90-second face / neck / wrist contact. Vagal-tone shift drops sympathetic arousal within seconds.

03 / STILLNESS

5-4-3-2-1 ጸጥታ

Sensory grounding ladder. Forces conscious attention through five real-world inputs the screen cannot fake.

Why three options: a single forced modality is a tax patients learn to avoid. Choice within constraint preserves agency without offering an exit.

SECTION 02 / SOLUTION · PHASE 02
09 / 16
→PHASE 02 / 3:00 — 8:00

Cognitive hijack.

The craving has been physically interrupted. Now the patient must re-engage the pre-frontal cortex — through self-confrontation or a validated focus task.
A / LETTER

Read what they wrote

The patient's own onboarding letter to their future self. 8-second forced read gate. No skip.

B / VOICE

Hear themselves

A 30-second voice memo recorded in calm. Must play to completion. The clinician's patient, talking to the patient in crisis.

C / COGNITIVE

Prove it

A focus task validated by multimodal AI (Gemma). Camera or text proof-of-work. Re-tries on failure. No workaround.

After minute 4, an "urge passed" link appears — but the patient must rate the urge 0–10 and complete a guided breath before exit. Rating ≥4 returns them silently to the task.

→WHAT MAKES IT CLINICAL

Proof of work. Not promises.

QUM is not a timer with affirmations. Every phase produces a cryptographic-or-multimodal artifact the system can verify.
CONTROL SURFACE
  • L01Session lock — wave state persisted to native storage. Force-close mid-wave logs failure on resume.
  • L02No abandon UI — phases gate on real elapsed time, not user input. The clock is the authority.
  • L03Exit check — early release requires urge re-rating, breath, and a 0–10 score. Rating ≥4 returns silently to task.
  • L04Tap-count verifier — physical modalities count real device touches; difficulty scales with resilience score.
AI VALIDATION LAYER
  • V01Gemma multimodal validates cognitive proof-of-work — camera capture or text response against task prompt.
  • V02Edge function arbiter — validation runs server-side via validate‑pow; client cannot self-approve.
  • V03Retry on failure, not skip. The patient cannot guess past the task.
  • V04No biometric storage. Validation results are stored; capture artefacts are not.

SECTION 04 / LOCAL VIABILITY
14 / 16
→BUILT FOR THE NETWORK YOU ACTUALLY HAVE

Engineered for Ethiopian conditions, not ported.

QUM degrades gracefully when the connection drops — which it will. The wave runs end-to-end without a server round-trip.
OFFLINE-FIRST ARCHITECTURE
  • A1Local task cache. Physical, cognitive and mindful prompts ship with the app. No network needed to start a wave.
  • A2Wave queue. Start, complete, fail events persist to native storage and flush when connectivity returns.
  • A3Reflections outbox. Voice notes and text submissions queue locally; nothing is lost in a dropout.
  • A4Session lock survives reboot. Force-close, kill, low-battery shutdown — the wave still resolves correctly on resume.
CULTURAL & LANGUAGE FIT
  • B1Amharic‑native brand. ቁም reads as imperative, not import. The product belongs to the language.
  • B2Non-shaming clinical posture. No interrogation. No streaks lost. Aligns with relational recovery norms.
  • B3Android-first. Capacitor build, tested on low-RAM devices. Camera, notifications, native storage.
  • B4Data sovereignty. Reflections and voice memos stay in encrypted patient buckets. Never ad-monetised.
