# Changelog

## Unreleased

QUM no longer punishes failure. It tracks practice, surfaces your own past commitments, and trusts you to do the work in your own time.

### Philosophy

- **No failure penalty** — stepping away from a wave logs the session but does not reduce resilience. Inactivity alone slowly softens the shield; practice restores it.
- **No interrogation** — reflections are voluntary, delayed, and skippable; nothing forces documentation right after a hard moment.
- **Your words, your timing** — letters and voice memos surface in waves as reminders you wrote for yourself, not as judgment from the app.
- **Anonymous solidarity** — you can see that others are surfing too, without exposing anyone’s identity.
- **Honest limits** — when signals suggest real distress, a single optional support card points to verified help; it dismisses cleanly and never auto-routes.

### Highlights

- Three-way Phase 1: Body, Cold, or Stillness
- Phase 2: cognitive proof-of-work, letter, or voice memo (randomized when available)
- Commitment override requires a unique 80+ character explanation
- Delayed reflection prompt ~5 hours after a failed wave
- Crisis support card + always-available Find support in Settings
- Consolidated Settings: letter, voice memo, reflection history, calm-hour schedule, account
